# That One Session That Got Me ₹500

So I attended this online session about browsers through something called Kactii AI Academy. I wasn't expecting much honestly. It's a platform where students learn stuff and also teach each other. The teaching part is what gets you credits.

It's called SSH — Student Side Hustle. Basically you learn something, then you teach it to others, and the platform gives you credits you can actually use. Simple as that.

My session was about browsers. Like what actually happens when you type a URL, what cookies are, developer tools and stuff. Things I never really thought about before even though I'm on a browser all day. I just attended, asked some questions, stayed till the end — and got ₹500 worth of credits just for participating.

I found out about this through my friend Kaaviya. She was already using it and just told me to try it. Glad I listened.

Honestly before I joined I was kind of nervous. Like what if everyone there is way smarter than me and I just sit there understanding nothing. That feeling where you don't want to ask questions because you think it'll sound dumb. I almost didn't join because of that.

But then I did. And it was actually fine. Nobody made me feel stupid for asking basic stuff. Someone explained cookies in a way that finally made sense to me. I didn't know what the Network tab in DevTools even was before that session. Now I kind of do.

The ₹500 was cool, not gonna lie. But what actually stayed with me was this weird feeling after the session ended. Like I felt a little smarter than I did an hour before. Not in a big dramatic way. Just quietly. Like okay, I get this now.

I think that's the thing about Kactii that surprised me. It doesn't feel like school. Nobody's testing you or grading you. You're just there because you want to be. And somehow that makes you actually pay attention.
The bigger thing for me was realizing you don't need to be super smart or an expert to be useful in a learning community. You just have to show up and actually engage.
Cool things happen when you just say yes sometimes.
