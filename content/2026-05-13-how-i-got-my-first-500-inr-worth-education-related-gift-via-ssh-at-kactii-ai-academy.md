# That One Session That Got Me ₹500

So I attended this online session about browsers through something called Kactii AI Academy. I wasn't expecting much honestly. It's a platform where students learn stuff and also teach each other. The teaching part is what gets you credits.

It's called SSH — Student Side Hustle. Basically you learn something, then you teach it to others, and the platform gives you credits you can actually use. Simple as that.

My session was about browsers. Like what actually happens when you type a URL, what cookies are, developer tools and stuff. Things I never really thought about before even though I'm on a browser all day. I just attended, asked some questions, stayed till the end — and got ₹500 worth of credits just for participating.

I found out about this through my friend Kaaviya. She was already using it and just told me to try it. Glad I listened.

Honestly the ₹500 was cool but the bigger thing for me was realizing you don't need to be super smart or an expert to be useful in a learning community. You just have to show up and actually engage.

Cool things happen when you just say yes sometimes.
