## What Is SSH? (Student Side Hustle)

SSH stands for Student Side Hustle — and the name says it all.

It's Kactii AI Academy's credit-based reward system designed specifically for students. Here's how it works:

* 👉 **Come** — Show up, be present, engage with the community
* 👉 **Join** — Participate in sessions, workshops, and events
* 👉 **Learn** — Absorb knowledge from peers and mentors
* 👉 **Teach** — Share what you know, take sessions, and help others grow
* 💰 **Get Credits** — Every meaningful contribution earns you credits that translate into real value

SSH turns your academic hustle into something tangible. It's not just about certificates on a wall — it's about building a track record of genuine participation and rewarding you for it.

Here's where it gets personal.

I'll be honest — I almost didn't join. That familiar feeling crept in: *what if everyone there is way smarter than me? What if I ask something and it sounds dumb?* I nearly talked myself out of it.

But I showed up anyway. And it changed things.

The session was all about browsers — the kind of stuff I'd never stopped to think about even though I'm staring at a browser all day. We went through things like:

* **What actually happens when you type a URL** — the full journey from address bar to loaded page
* **Cookies** — what they are, why they exist, and why everyone keeps asking you to accept them
* **Developer Tools** — especially the Network tab, which I genuinely had no idea what to do with before
* **Practical browser concepts** every tech student should understand but rarely does

It was a session I attended as a learner, not an expert. I just showed up, asked questions, and stayed engaged till the end.

The community was warm. Nobody made me feel stupid for asking basic stuff. Someone explained cookies in a way that finally clicked for me. And my participation was recognized through the SSH credit system.

**Result? I earned credits worth ₹500 — my first education-related reward ever.**

That ₹500 wasn't just money. It was that quiet feeling after the session ended — *I'm a little smarter than I was an hour ago.* Not in a big, dramatic way. Just steadily. Like: okay, I get this now.
